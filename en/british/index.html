🧑‍🤝‍🧑 British

People

Also known as: Britons, British people

Cheddar and Stilton; the tradition of tea with milk.

Britain, with its mild, damp climate and green pastures, is cow country. Well-known dairy breeds were developed on the islands — Jersey, Guernsey and Ayrshire.

Cheddar, a hard cheese named after the village of Cheddar in Somerset, is today the best-known British cheese. It is made all over the world, but “West Country Farmhouse Cheddar” from south-west England is protected as a designation of origin. Stilton, a blue cheese from central England, is protected too. Lancashire, Wensleydale and Cheshire are also well known.

A distinctly British habit is tea with milk. It spread in the 18th–19th centuries, when tea became a mass drink; arguing over whether to pour the milk before or after the tea is part of everyday culture. A traditional cream tea is tea with scones, clotted cream and jam.

Frequently asked questions

Why do the British put milk in their tea?

The habit took shape in the 18th–19th centuries, when tea became a mass drink; milk softens its strength.

Where does the name cheddar come from?

From the village of Cheddar in the county of Somerset.

What is a cream tea?

Tea with scones, thick clotted cream and jam.
