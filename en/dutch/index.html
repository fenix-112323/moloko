🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Dutch

People

Also known as: Dutch people, Netherlanders

Gouda and Edam, cheeses known all over the world.

The Netherlands is a low-lying country with a damp maritime climate and lush meadows on reclaimed land, ideal for cows. The Dutch black-and-white cattle became the basis of the world’s most widespread dairy breed, the Holstein Friesian.

The Dutch have traded cheese for centuries: the cheese markets of Gouda, Alkmaar and Edam date back to the Middle Ages. Gouda is a semi-hard cow’s milk cheese sold anywhere from young to aged for several years; Edam is a round cheese in a red wax coating. “Gouda Holland” and “Noord-Hollandse Gouda” are protected in the EU.

Milk and cheese are part of everyday Dutch food: a cheese sandwich for lunch with a glass of milk or buttermilk is a familiar combination. Today the Netherlands is one of the world’s largest cheese exporters.

Frequently asked questions

Where does the name Gouda come from?

From the town of Gouda, which has long held a cheese market.

Why is Edam cheese red?

It is coated in red wax to protect it during storage and transport.

Which dairy breed comes from the Netherlands?

Dutch black-and-white cattle formed the basis of the Holstein Friesian breed.
