🍵 Tea with milk

Dish/drink

Also known as: milk tea

England, Asia.

Tea with milk is drunk in many countries. In Britain, most tea is drunk with cold milk. That is why it is simply called “tea” there, with no extra word. Usually it is strong black tea with a little milk added.

Asia has many drinks of its own. In India and Pakistan, “chai” almost always means tea with milk. Masala chai is brewed with milk, sugar and spices. In Hong Kong, milk tea appeared back in British times. Local people found it weak, so they began using strong Ceylon tea and unsweetened condensed milk. In Mongolia people drink suutei tsai, a salty tea with milk. In Central Asia, tea with milk is poured into small bowls called piala.

Milk makes tea softer and less bitter. It cools the drink a little and makes it more filling.

Frequently asked questions

Where do people drink tea with milk most?

In Britain, India, Pakistan, Hong Kong, Mongolia and Central Asia.

Why put milk in tea?

Milk softens the taste, takes away the bitterness and makes tea more filling.
