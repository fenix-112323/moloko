🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Xhosa

People

Also known as: amaXhosa, Xhosa people

A bride is welcomed with amasi as a sign of acceptance into her new family.

The Xhosa (amaXhosa) are a people of the Eastern Cape province of South Africa. As with their neighbours, cattle were historically a measure of wealth and part of marriage and family rituals, and dairy products were everyday food.

The main traditional dairy product is amasi, a thick sour milk. Raw cow’s milk is soured in a calabash or leather bag, and the whey is then drained off. Amasi is eaten with maize porridge or drunk. It is linked to a wedding custom: according to accounts, a bride arriving at her husband’s home is served amasi with a piece of meat as a sign of her acceptance into the new family. Details of the ritual may vary between communities.

Today amasi is a mass-market product throughout southern Africa, made industrially with starter cultures. Home souring is still practised, especially in rural areas.

Frequently asked questions

What is amasi for the Xhosa?

A thick sour milk made from raw cow’s milk; it is eaten with maize porridge.

What Xhosa wedding custom involves amasi?

According to accounts, the bride is served amasi with a piece of meat as a sign of her acceptance into the new family.

Where do the Xhosa people live?

Mainly in the Eastern Cape province of South Africa.
