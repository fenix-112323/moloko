🧑‍🍳 Pastry chef

Job

Also known as: confectioner

Works with cream, butter and condensed milk.

A pastry chef makes cakes, pastries, biscuits, sweets and other desserts. In a restaurant, the pastry chef runs a separate pastry section of the kitchen. In French this person is called a pâtissier. In a bakery or a factory, pastry cooks make sweets in large batches using machines.

Dairy products are the basis of pastry work. Cream is whipped into a topping. Butter is needed for puff pastry and shortcrust pastry and for buttercreams. Condensed milk goes into creams and fillings, curd cheese into cheesecakes and cheese pastries, and milk into custard and dough. The result depends a lot on the quality of the cream and butter.

The work needs precision. A pastry chef weighs ingredients, mixes them, rolls out and shapes dough. They watch the oven temperature and check the finished products. In classic restaurant kitchens, pastry work has its own specialists: for baking, for sweets, for cake decorating and for cold and frozen desserts. A pastry chef often plans the dessert menu and comes up with new recipes.

Frequently asked questions

What dairy products does a pastry chef use?

Cream for whipping, butter for dough and creams, condensed milk, curd cheese and milk.

What is the difference between a pastry chef and a baker?

A baker mainly bakes bread and baked goods, while a pastry chef specialises in cakes, pastries, desserts and sweets.

What is a pastry chef called in France?

A pâtissier; a female pastry chef is a pâtissière.
