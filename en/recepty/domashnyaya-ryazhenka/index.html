Homemade ryazhenka

How to make it at home: Homemade ryazhenka — ingredients, steps, safety tips.

Time: about 10 hours (3.5–4 hours slow cooking + 5–6 hours fermenting) · Yield: about 4 litres (two 2-litre jars)

Ingredients

  • 5 litres full-fat milk
  • 1 cup sour cream

Method

  1. Wash your hands, the pan and the glass jars.
  2. Pour the milk into a large pan, half-cover with a lid and cook over very low heat for 3.5–4 hours.
  3. Every 20–30 minutes, stir and break up the skin in the centre so that it sinks.
  4. The milk should turn creamy and thicken slightly.
  5. Pour the milk into the jars, stir in the sour cream and cover loosely with lids.
  6. Wrap in a towel and leave in a warm kitchen: the milk will ferment in 5–6 hours (it can be left overnight).
  7. In the morning, stir, seal tightly and refrigerate.

Tips

  • Keep in the fridge for up to 5 days.
  • Use fresh sour cream with live cultures: it acts as the starter.
  • Quick version: ferment ready-made baked milk; in commercial ryazhenka, fermentation takes 3–6 hours.
