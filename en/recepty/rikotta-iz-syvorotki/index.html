Whey ricotta

How to make it at home: Whey ricotta — ingredients, steps, safety tips.

Time: about 1 hour (plus cooling) · Yield: depends on how fresh the whey is and how much milk is added

Ingredients

  • 3.8 litres fresh whey from making cultured cheese
  • optional: whole milk, 5–12% of the whey volume
  • ½ tsp salt
  • optional: ½ tsp citric acid dissolved in ½ cup water

Method

  1. The fresher the whey, the better the result; work with clean hands and equipment.
  2. Heat the whey slowly without stirring. At 71 °C you can add milk for yield and flavour.
  3. At 77 °C, add the salt and stir quickly.
  4. Keep heating without stirring to 85 °C (up to 88–93 °C is fine; no need to boil) and wait for the curds to rise.
  5. If there are few curds, pour in the citric acid solution and stir vigorously for 5–10 seconds; white curds and clear, greenish whey are the sign of success.
  6. Use a slotted spoon to gently push the curds towards the centre and let them rest for 10–15 minutes.
  7. Transfer the curds to moulds or a sieve: drain briefly for soft ricotta, or for several hours for firm ricotta.
  8. Cool below 10 °C and keep in the fridge.

Tips

  • Too much acid makes the curds heavy (they sink) and the cheese less sweet.
  • Eat the ricotta within 10 days.
  • Take care when pouring the hot whey.
