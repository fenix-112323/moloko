The journey of milk

How milk travels from a cow on the pasture to the buyer's fridge: feeding, milking, cooling, transport, processing at the dairy, bottling, warehouse and shop.

  1. 🌿 Grass — The main summer feed. Feed shapes the taste and composition of milk.
  2. 🌄 Pasture — A meadow where the herd grazes.
  3. 🔄 Grazing — The herd grazes on pasture.
  4. 🐄 Cow — The world's main source of milk: ≈83% of global milk output (FAO, 2010). Dairy breeds give 20–40 L a day.
  5. 🔄 Milking — 2–3 times a day, by hand or by machine.
  6. 🔄 Filtration — Removing debris right after milking.
  7. ❄️ Cooling — To +4 °C to stop bacterial growth.
  8. ⚙️ Bulk milk cooler — A farm tank that chills milk to +4 °C within 2 hours of milking.
  9. 🚛 Milk tanker — An insulated tanker for transporting raw milk.
  10. 🚛 Raw milk transport — Farm → dairy plant by milk tanker, usually within a day.
  11. 🔄 Milk reception — The plant receives the milk and takes samples.
  12. ✅ Quality control — Fat, protein, bacteria, antibiotics, acidity.
  13. 🔄 Separation — Splitting into cream and skimmed milk.
  14. 🔄 Standardization — Fat content is adjusted to 1.5 / 2.5 / 3.2%.
  15. 🔄 Homogenization — Breaking up fat globules so the cream doesn't separate.
  16. 🌡️ Pasteurization — 72–76 °C for 15–20 seconds: kills pathogens; keeps ~5–10 days chilled.
  17. 🔄 Filling — Packing into containers.
  18. 🏷️ Labelling — Date, expiry date, ingredients, batch code.
  19. 📦 Warehouse — Storage of finished products before dispatch.
  20. 🚚 Distribution — Plant → warehouse → distribution center → store.
  21. 📦 Distribution center — A retail chain's large warehouse from which goods are delivered to stores.
  22. 🏪 Shop — The point of sale, where milk sits in a chilled display.
  23. 🚚 Delivery — From the store or straight to the shopper's home.
  24. 🛒 Shopper — The end point of the chain: picks milk off the shelf.
  25. 🧊 Refrigerator — Home storage of milk: no warmer than +4 °C.
