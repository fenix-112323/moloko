🧑‍🤝‍🧑 French

People

Also known as: French people

Hundreds of cheese varieties: Camembert, Roquefort, Brie, Comté.

France has one of the richest cheese cultures in the world: hundreds of cheeses are made here from cow’s, sheep’s and goat’s milk. The variety reflects the landscape, from the wet pastures of Normandy to the Jura and Alps and the limestone plateaus of the south.

Roquefort, a blue sheep’s milk cheese, is aged in caves near the village of Roquefort-sur-Soulzon, where the noble mold grows. It is one of the first protected names in Europe. Camembert and Brie are soft, white-rind cow’s milk cheeses; Comté and Beaufort are hard cheeses from mountain dairies; chèvre is a family of goat cheeses.

In France cheese is a course of its own: it is served after the main course, with bread. Many cheeses are protected as designations of origin (AOP), which supports farmhouse cheesemaking.

Frequently asked questions

How many types of cheese are there in France?

Hundreds; the exact number depends on how you count the varieties.

What milk is Roquefort made from?

Sheep’s milk; the cheese is aged in the natural caves of Roquefort-sur-Soulzon.

What does AOP mean on cheese?

Protected Designation of Origin: the cheese was made in a specific area according to set rules.
