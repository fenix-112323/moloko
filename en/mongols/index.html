🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Mongols

People

Also known as: Mongolians

Airag (kumis) and the dairy foods tsagaan idee, "white food".

For Mongols, many of whom still move with their herds today, dairy products are the basis of the summer diet. They are called tsagaan idee, “white food”. In Mongolian culture white symbolises purity and well-being, so white food opens festivals and the welcoming of guests.

The main drink is airag, kumis made from mare’s milk. It is made in a leather vessel called a khukhuur, with the milk churned hundreds of times with a paddle; the starter is carefully kept going from year to year. Airag is drunk every day, offered at the Naadam festival and used in rituals of blessing and offerings. In 2019 UNESCO added this technique to its intangible heritage list.

Besides mares, Mongols milk cows, yaks, sheep, goats and camels. Milk is used to make tarag (sour milk), urum (clotted cream), aarts and aaruul — dried curd — as well as the milk vodka arkhi. In summer, guests are welcomed precisely with dairy food.

Frequently asked questions

What is tsagaan idee?

“White food”, the general Mongolian name for dairy products; it symbolises purity and opens festive meals.

What is the difference between airag and kumis?

They are the same thing — a drink of fermented mare’s milk; airag is the Mongolian name.

What is aaruul?

Dried curd that is made in summer and stored for the winter.
