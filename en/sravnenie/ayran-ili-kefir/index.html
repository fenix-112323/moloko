Ayran vs kefir: what's the difference

Both are fermented milk drinks, but ayran is usually diluted with water and salted, while kefir is drunk undiluted. Their starters differ too.

🍶 Ayran🥛 Kefir
StarterLactobacillus bulgaricus and Streptococcus thermophilusKefir grains: bacteria and yeasts
How it's madeKatyk (fermented milk) is mixed with cold boiled, spring or mineral water and saltMilk is fermented with kefir grains; not diluted with water
FermentationLacticLactic and alcoholic (a fraction of a percent of alcohol)
TasteLike salted kefir with a hint of wheyTangy, unsalted, sometimes slightly fizzy
Raw materialUsually cow's milk, sometimes goat's or sheep'sCow's milk
OriginCentral and Western Asia, the Caucasus, the Balkans; cuisines of Turkic and Caucasian peoplesNorth Caucasus

Which to choose

In hot weather and with meat dishes, salty ayran is more refreshing. If you need an unsalted drink for breakfast or for baking, choose kefir.
