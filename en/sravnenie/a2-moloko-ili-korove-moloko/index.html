A2 milk vs regular milk: what's the difference

A2 milk is cow's milk in which beta-casein is present only as the A2 variant. Regular milk from Western cattle breeds often contains both variants, A1 and A2.

🥛 A2 milk🥛 Cow's milk
ProteinOnly A2 beta-casein: the amino acid proline at position 67Often a mix of A1 and A2: A1 has histidine at position 67
DigestionThe BCM-7 peptide is not cleaved from A2 at this siteEnzymes can cleave the BCM-7 peptide from A1; its formation in humans is not confirmed by all studies
CowsSpecially selected herds; over 70% of Guernsey cows give mainly A2 milk; African and Asian cattle produce only A2In Holstein and Ayrshire cows, 46–70% of animals give milk with both A1 and A2
Health benefitsEFSA (2009): no cause-and-effect relationship between BCM-7 and non-communicable diseases has been establishedEFSA found no link between drinking A1 milk and disease
MarketA2 Corporation was founded in New Zealand in 2000 (The a2 Milk Company since 2014)Ordinary mass-produced milk

Which to choose

There is no scientific evidence yet of any special benefit from A2 milk, although some people subjectively find it easier to tolerate. It is not lactose-free milk: if you are lactose intolerant, choose lactose-free milk.
