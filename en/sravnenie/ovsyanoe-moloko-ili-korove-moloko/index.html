Oat milk vs cow milk: what's the difference

Oat "milk" is a plant-based drink made from oats and water, not animal milk. The two are similar in calories but differ greatly in protein, fat and composition.

🌱 Oat milk🥛 Cow's milk
Raw materialOats and waterCow's milk
How it's madeOats are ground and mixed with warm water; enzymes and heat turn the slurry into a base, which is separated from the solids, homogenized and pasteurized or sterilizedCooled, clarified, standardized and pasteurized
Calories (per cup)About 120 kcal (unsweetened)About 149 kcal (whole)
Protein and fatAbout 40% of the protein, 63% of the fat and 10% of the saturated fat of cow's milkMore protein and saturated fat
Carbohydrates and fibreAbout 1.5 times more carbohydrates and roughly half the sugars; about 2 g of fibreNo fibre; the main sugar is lactose
Vitamins and mineralsUnfortified, it is lower in calcium, iron and vitamin A, so these are often addedA natural source of calcium
HistoryDeveloped by Swedish scientist Rickard Öste at Lund University in the 1990sDrunk for thousands of years

Which to choose

Oat drink works well in coffee, for people with a cow milk protein allergy or lactose intolerance, and for those who avoid animal products. If you need protein, choose cow's milk or fortified drinks and read the label.
