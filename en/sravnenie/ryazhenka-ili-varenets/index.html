Ryazhenka vs varenets: what's the difference

Both drinks are made from baked milk, which gives them a creamy colour and a caramel note. The difference lies in how far the milk is reduced and what it is fermented with.

🧀 Ryazhenka🧀 Varenets
Raw materialBaked cow's milkReduced baked milk: cooked down by at least a third, to a reddish tint
StarterThermophilic lactic streptococci and Lactobacillus bulgaricusAt home, sour cream (about 200 g per litre); in production, thermophilic streptococci
Fermentation time3–6 hours3–4 hours, covered, in a warm place
Taste and colourCreamy colour, mild caramel flavourDarker, with the rich taste of reduced milk
VariationsAt home, sometimes baked in the oven until a brown skin formsIn the Urals and Siberia it was fermented with cream and served with tea
OriginA traditional food of East Slavic cuisinesThe same; related to Turkic katyk made from long-boiled milk

Which to choose

If you like a mild, creamy taste, choose ryazhenka. If you prefer a richer caramel note and a thicker body, try varenets.
