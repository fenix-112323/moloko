⚙️ Filling line

Equipment

Also known as: bottling line

Automatically packs milk into bottles and cartons.

A filling line is a chain of machines that fills cartons, bottles or cups with finished milk, seals them and prints the labelling. A large line runs almost without people. Operators and engineers watch the settings, packaging changes and cleaning.

For pasteurised milk, the key things are cleanliness and cold. The milk arrives chilled from a buffer tank and goes straight into a cold room after packing. UHT milk needs an aseptic line. Aseptic means “free of germs”: the product and the packaging are sterilised separately, then brought together and sealed in a sterile environment. Only this way can milk keep for months without a fridge.

The Swedish company Tetra Pak installed the first machine for filling cardboard tetrahedrons in 1952. The aseptic version appeared in 1961, and the rectangular “brick” carton in 1963. Packaging not only holds the milk but also protects it from light and oxygen. Light destroys vitamins and gives an off taste, so an opaque carton protects milk better than a clear bottle.

Frequently asked questions

What is aseptic filling?

It is packing where the product and the packaging are each sterilised separately, then brought together and sealed in a sterile environment. UHT milk is filled this way.

Why is milk usually sold in opaque cartons?

Light destroys milk's vitamins and gives it an off taste. Cardboard protects from light better than clear glass or plastic.
