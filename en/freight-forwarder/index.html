📋 Freight forwarder

Job

Also known as: forwarding agent

Accompanies the cargo and handles the paperwork on delivery.

A freight forwarder arranges the transport of goods and handles the paperwork. They usually do not drive the truck themselves. Their job is to agree with the carrier, check that the goods are loaded correctly, and hand them over to the receiver with the right documents.

In the dairy industry, a forwarder works with refrigerated trucks, which have a cooling unit. They check that the truck body is cooled to the right temperature. They count the boxes and check the delivery notes, the documents that say what is being carried and how much. When the goods are handed over to a warehouse or shop, they complete the receiving paperwork. If packaging is damaged or the temperature is wrong, they record it in the documents. For shipments to other countries, the forwarder also prepares customs papers.

The profession is old. The first international freight forwarders are thought to be London innkeepers of the early 19th century. They stored their guests' belongings and sent them on. Later, companies appeared that arranged the transport of goods by rail and by steamship. The International Federation of Freight Forwarders Associations (FIATA) calls the forwarder the “architect of transport”.

Frequently asked questions

What does a freight forwarder do?

Arranges the transport of goods, checks the loading and keeps the goods covered by documents until they are handed to the receiver.

What is the difference between a freight forwarder and a driver?

The driver drives the truck, while the forwarder is responsible for the goods themselves and the paperwork. Sometimes one person does both jobs.

What does a forwarder check when transporting dairy products?

The temperature in the refrigerated truck, the number and condition of the packs, and that the delivery notes are correct.
