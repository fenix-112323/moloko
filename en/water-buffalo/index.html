🐃 Water buffalo

Animal

Also known as: buffalo, Bubalus bubalis

India, Pakistan, Italy. Rich milk; the classic is mozzarella di bufala.

This is the domestic water buffalo: according to the FAO, buffalo provide about 15% of the world’s milk — more than all goats, sheep and camels combined. Over 98% of buffalo live in Asia; in India and Pakistan more buffalo milk is produced than cow’s milk, and in Pakistan and Nepal there are more milking buffalo than milking cows.

There are river buffalo (the main dairy breeds of South Asia) and swamp buffalo, which are smaller and give less milk. A river buffalo usually gives 1,500–4,500 liters per lactation. Buffalo stay productive for a long time, up to 20 years, although they first calve late and the intervals between calvings are long.

Buffalo milk is rich (often 6–8% fat or more) and white, without a yellowish tint. In South Asia it is made into ghee, paneer and sweets, and in Italy’s Campania into mozzarella di bufala, protected in the EU.

Frequently asked questions

How is buffalo milk different from cow’s milk?

It is richer and denser, with more protein and solids, so it yields more cheese and butter.

How much milk does a buffalo give?

A river buffalo usually gives 1,500–4,500 liters per lactation, that is, several liters a day on average.

What milk is real mozzarella di bufala made from?

Buffalo milk: Mozzarella di Bufala Campana is a protected name tied to Campania and neighboring areas of Italy.
